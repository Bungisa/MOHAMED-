ElimikaPDF — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 · Last Updated: November 10, 2026
100% On-Device Local Privacy
Summary of Our Privacy Commitment:

ElimikaPDF is a local-first PDF Reader, Document Scanner, PDF Editor, Word Converter, and Optical Character Recognition (OCR) application. Your documents, camera scans, PDF annotations, and extracted text are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, collect, or transmit your personal documents to any external tracking or advertising servers.

1. Information We Collect and Process

ElimikaPDF is designed to operate without requiring user registration or personal accounts. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, phone number, physical address, or payment information.

  • Documents & Files: Any PDF files, Word documents (.docx), or images you open, edit, merge, split, compress, or convert are processed locally inside your device's memory and stored in your device's local IndexedDB storage vault.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Text recognition (English and Kiswahili) runs locally on your device. Extracted text remains inside your local document vault unless you explicitly export or share it.

2. Device Permissions We Request

To provide core document scanning and PDF management features, ElimikaPDF may request the following permissions on your device:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly when you open the Document Scanner to photograph physical pages, receipts, notes, or contracts for perspective cropping and PDF creation. Camera frames are processed in real time on your device and are never uploaded or streamed to external servers.
  • File & Media Access: Used only when you explicitly pick a PDF, Word file, or image from your device storage (or share a PDF to ElimikaPDF via the system Share / Open With menu) and when you save or download edited files back to your device.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used only if you enable local notifications for completed document tasks.

3. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

  • On-Device Storage: All stored documents and user preferences (such as Light/Dark theme and default scan filters) are saved locally on your device using browser IndexedDB and localStorage.
  • Full User Control & Instant Deletion: You can delete any individual document at any time by tapping the Delete (trash) icon next to the file, or erase all locally stored data at once by navigating to Settings → Delete All Locally Stored Documents (or by clearing the app data / uninstalling the app on your device).

4. Third-Party Sharing & Analytics

ElimikaPDF does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or data brokers. Your files are only shared with other applications (such as WhatsApp, Gmail, or Google Drive) when you explicitly tap the Share or Send to Email button on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

ElimikaPDF is a general-audience productivity and educational document utility. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to ElimikaPDF or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
